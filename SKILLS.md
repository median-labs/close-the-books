# The seventeen skills

Each is a folder under [`skills/`](skills/) holding one `SKILL.md`, and each
stands on its own. You can reach for `reconcile` on books that are otherwise
fine, or `book-a-safe` on a single instrument in the wrong place, without running
anything else. Most people arrive with a year that stopped and a return due, and
for that the order below is the one to follow: it is the order a real catch-up
runs, which is not alphabetical and does matter.

If your agent cannot load skills, [`PLAYBOOK.md`](PLAYBOOK.md) carries the same
procedure in one file.

## Before any work starts

These four decide what the work is. Skipping them does not save time, it moves
the cost to the end, where a wrong answer is expensive.

| # | Skill | What it does | Reach for it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`learn-my-books`](skills/learn-my-books/SKILL.md) | Reads the QuickBooks exports, assigns every account a role, and mines what-goes-where rules from the company's own posted history | Always first. Nothing else knows anything about the company until this has run |
| 2 | [`read-the-screen`](skills/read-the-screen/SKILL.md) | Collects the five facts no export carries, by naming the screen and what to read off it, and records each with the date it was given | Right after `learn`. Four of those five decide what a catch-up even consists of |
| 3 | [`stop-the-automation`](skills/stop-the-automation/SKILL.md) | Turns off the rules still posting into the year being filed, and finds what they already posted with nobody watching | Before any figure is produced. Every hour worked before the rules stop is worked twice |
| 4 | [`scope-the-year`](skills/scope-the-year/SKILL.md) | Splits the queue into what this deadline needs and what belongs to next year or to a month that already reconciled | Whenever a For Review count is being quoted as the size of the job |

## The main sequence

| # | Skill | What it does | Reach for it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | [`untangle-duplicate-accounts`](skills/untangle-duplicate-accounts/SKILL.md) | Handles one real account that appears twice after a re-link, in the order that does not destroy evidence | Two accounts have almost the same name, or the history and the feed are on different rows |
| 6 | [`tie-out-statements`](skills/tie-out-statements/SKILL.md) | Asks for the statements the books need, by account and month with a reason for each, then proves each month's transactions are complete | Before categorizing anything. Its first half runs before you have a single statement |
| 7 | [`prove-the-period-is-complete`](skills/prove-the-period-is-complete/SKILL.md) | Takes the book balance, adds everything unbooked, and compares the result to what the bank says it holds | Before calling a queue finished, and whenever a feed may have stopped |
| 8 | [`ask-the-founder`](skills/ask-the-founder/SKILL.md) | Turns what the books cannot settle into a short, specific list of questions, and records the answers with a date and a source | Whenever a categorization, an entry or a treatment is blocked on something only the owner knows |
| 9 | [`catch-up`](skills/catch-up/SKILL.md) | Works the in-scope backlog into reviewable batches: add, match, transfer or question, one row at a time, with the evidence for each | The main event. Books are months or years behind |
| 10 | [`fill-feed-gaps`](skills/fill-feed-gaps/SKILL.md) | Builds bank upload files from statements for the months a dead feed missed | A feed stopped, an account was connected late, or reconnecting only brought back 90 days |
| 11 | [`post-recurring-entries`](skills/post-recurring-entries/SKILL.md) | Drafts the repeating adjusting entries with no bank transaction behind them: payroll, prepaid, intangibles, processor revenue | After the backlog is categorized, before reconciling |
| 12 | [`reconcile`](skills/reconcile/SKILL.md) | Book balance against statement balance, every account, every month, with the difference stated including the zeros | After the entries are in, before declaring a period closed |
| 13 | [`check-the-books`](skills/check-the-books/SKILL.md) | The ten exit tests, each reporting the number it measured rather than a tick, plus every account holding a balance on the side opposite its normal one | After categorizing and reconciling, before the books go anywhere |
| 14 | [`check-before-filing`](skills/check-before-filing/SKILL.md) | Packages the books with the evidence for every figure: evidence ledger, change log, exit tests, archive | The last step before the books leave the company |
| 15 | [`ready-to-file`](skills/ready-to-file/SKILL.md) | Works out what the firm filing the return still has to be given, collects it, and refuses to call the books ready while anything is unrecorded | The terminus. Finished bookkeeping and a return that can be signed are different states |

## The two that come up on their own

| Skill | What it does | Reach for it when |
|---|---|---|
| [`book-a-safe`](skills/book-a-safe/SKILL.md) | Works out where a convertible instrument belongs on the balance sheet and drafts the reclassification if it is in the wrong place | An instrument sits in equity, a raise is not booked, or a return, audit or diligence is coming |
| [`wind-down`](skills/wind-down/SKILL.md) | Drafts the closing entries for a dissolution, and refuses until the facts they rest on are answered | A company is shutting down and its final books and final return have to be right |

## How they fit together

`learn-my-books` writes the profile every other skill reads. `read-the-screen`
adds the five things it cannot know, because no export carries the bank's own
balance, the For Review count, the bank rules, whether a feed is connected, or
who authored an entry.

`scope-the-year` is what turns a queue depth into a number of items. On the file
these skills were rebuilt around, 863 items on the tab were 312 items of work,
and 197 of the rest were the feed offering back transactions the books already
held.

`tie-out-statements` decides whether you have every transaction you were sent.
`prove-the-period-is-complete` decides whether you were sent every transaction,
which is a different question and the one that catches a feed with a hole in it.

`catch-up` and `fill-feed-gaps` are the two that produce files for the owner to
upload, and both stop at the approval gate. `post-recurring-entries`,
`book-a-safe` and `wind-down` produce entries to type, because QuickBooks Online
in the United States cannot import journal entries.

`reconcile` and `check-the-books` are the evidence that the work is done.
`check-before-filing` is what makes that evidence checkable by someone who was
not here.

`ready-to-file` is the last one, and it asks a different question from all the
others: whether the firm that will file the return has everything it needs.
Its collecting half can start as soon as `learn-my-books` has run, because the
questions come out of the chart. Its gate half runs at the end and refuses while
anything a preparer would have to ask for has nothing recorded against it.

## The four refusals

These are gates rather than advice. Each names the reason it fired and the
command that clears it, and none of them takes a flag.

| Refusal | Why it is not a warning |
|---|---|
| Nothing about disconnecting or merging an account while its For Review queue holds anything | Disconnecting deletes those items, and on an account whose activity was never booked they are the only record of it in the file |
| The queue cannot be called finished while a completeness finding is open | Finishing the queue and the period being complete are different things, and the difference is invisible without the check |
| Nothing that produces figures for a return while rules are still posting into the year being filed | A figure about a file that is still changing is a figure about a moment that has passed |
| No set of books is called ready while something the firm filing the return would have to ask for is unrecorded | The chasing happens before the deadline or during it, and the answer costs the same either way. Where an answer will not come, `--cannot "why"` records that, which the preparer can work with |
